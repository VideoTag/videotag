True one-click downloads

Vidlens runs entirely in your browser with no server of its own. That's great for privacy — but a browser cannot pull a video stream from YouTube by itself: the stream URLs are cipher-signed and cross-origin blocked. Connect a downloader API and Vidlens does the rest in one click: fetch → save → attach to your notes → bundled into your Offline Pack.

Why can't you just use a public one? Public instances used to work. The main cobalt instance has now disabled YouTube ("restrictions from YouTube's side and infinite maintenance cost at scale"), and community instances are mostly offline or require a token. Your own instance is the only durable answer — and it costs nothing to run.

1Run an instance

cobalt is free and open source. On any machine with Docker (a €4/month VPS, a Raspberry Pi, or your own PC):

docker run -d --name cobalt-api -p 9000:9000 \
  -e API_URL="https://dl.yourdomain.com/" \
  --restart unless-stopped \
  ghcr.io/imputnet/cobalt:11

Put it behind HTTPS — a reverse proxy (Caddy, Nginx) or a free Cloudflare Tunnel if it's on a home machine. API_URL must match the public HTTPS address exactly. Full options: the official guide.

2Allow your browser to call it

Your instance must send permissive CORS headers so the page can talk to it (cobalt does this by default; if you put it behind a proxy, keep the header):

Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://vidlens.net

Prefer to lock it down? Use your own origin instead of * — the request is made by your browser, so only you need access.

3Connect it in Vidlens

Load a video, click Download, and paste your instance URL into the One-click download field. It's stored in your browser only (localStorage) and reused from then on. Pick a quality and hit Download & attach:

  • the file downloads to your computer,
  • it is attached to the current video in Vidlens,
  • your next Offline Pack bundles it automatically,
  • and it becomes the audio source for AI subtitle generation.

No server? Other routes

  • The platform's own download — YouTube Premium offline, YouTube Studio for your own uploads. Always the safest.
  • yt-dlp — a local tool, no website involved. Vidlens shows a ready-to-paste command.
  • A public downloader — the embedded one still handles TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Vimeo and more (just not YouTube).
  • Nothing at all — Vidlens works fine without the file: notes, exports and reports all function; only offline playback needs it.

Please download responsibly. Save videos you have the right to keep — your own uploads, Creative-Commons material, or content the platform lets you take offline. Respect creators and the terms of the services you use.
